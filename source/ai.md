# Penida: official reference

Penida is a French startup studio building SaaS products and Shopify apps. The studio was founded by Marc and Charles. Its website presents its products, team and practical articles for app builders.

Updated: September 23, 2026.

- Website: [Penida](https://penida.io)

## Portfolio updates

Goat Badges and Bee Logo Showcase have been sold and are no longer current Penida apps. Bee Logo Showcase is distinct from the Beez bundle and subscription products.
